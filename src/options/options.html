YouTube and Twitch

Forte works on YouTube and Twitch like on any other site. A video can only be boosted by one add-on at a time: if another add-on is already boosting it (YouTube/Twitch Enhancer's own volume boost, for example), that add-on keeps the video and Forte's popup tells you so. To keep Forte off a site, use the switch in its popup there, or the "Forte is off on" list below.

Players Forte leaves alone

Firefox gives add-ons a page's sound only when the audio comes from the same site, or from a server that allows it. For anything else, boosting would turn the player silent, so Forte checks first and leaves such players playing normally. The popup tells you when that happens. Forte also leaves alone players that another add-on or the site itself already processes, and it changes nothing at all until you raise the volume or pick an EQ preset.
